Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026. Side Work: Tips & Budget ("Side Work", "we") is made by Alliance Digital Studios, Utah, USA. Contact: hello@hellosidework.com.

The short version

  • No account is required to use Side Work.
  • Your shifts, tips, bills and budget stay on your phone, backed up to your own iCloud or Google Drive.
  • No ads. No tracking. We never sell your data.
  • If you choose to link a bank or pay card, we read deposits only, read-only, and delete them when you unlink.

Information stored on your device

Shifts, sales, tips, tip-outs, hours, bills, budget settings and your work profile are stored in an encrypted database on your device. We do not receive this information. If you turn on backup, an encrypted copy is saved to your own iCloud Drive or Google Drive account.

Optional bank linking (Plaid)

If you choose to link a bank or pay card, we use Plaid Inc. to connect. You sign in to your bank inside Plaid; we never see your bank username or password. With your permission we receive, for the accounts you select: transaction date, amount, pending status and the last four digits of the account. We discard merchant names and categories and keep deposits only, for up to 90 days, to match them to your shifts. Plaid's handling of your data is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy.

When bank linking is on, we create an account for you identified by a passkey, store the Plaid access token encrypted on our servers, and never share it. Unlinking removes the connection at Plaid and deletes the synced deposits within 24 hours. If you don't open the app for 90 days, we remove the connection automatically.

Diagnostics

If you opt in, we collect crash reports with dollar amounts, account numbers and notes removed. You can turn this off in Settings.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We don't move money. Side Work cannot make payments or transfers.

Your choices and rights

You can export all your data (CSV or PDF) or delete it from Settings at any time. To request access to or deletion of anything stored on our servers, email hello@hellosidework.com; we respond within 30 days. California and other state residents have rights to know, delete and opt out of sale; we do not sell personal information.

Security

Data on your device is encrypted and protected by Face ID or your passcode. Server data is encrypted at rest and in transit, isolated per user, and access is logged. Report security issues to security@hellosidework.com.

Children

Side Work is not intended for anyone under 17.

Changes

We'll post changes here and update the effective date. Material changes will be announced in the app.
